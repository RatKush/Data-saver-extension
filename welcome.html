Ads, images and video now stop at the network layer — before a single byte reaches your connection. Here's what that actually looks like.

Where a page's weight actually goes

typical page · share of total bytes
Images Video Ads Text
  • Images 30–60%
  • Video & audio 10–35%
  • Ads & trackers 10–30%
  • The actual page

Data Saver stops the first three before they download — which on most sites is the large majority of what you were about to pay for. Ranges are typical for the modern web and vary a lot by site; your own running total lives in the popup.

What's switched on

🚫 Ads & trackers

Ad networks, analytics and tracking scripts. Almost nobody turns this one off.

🖼️ Images

Every image, everywhere. The biggest saving — and the biggest visual change.

🎬 Video & audio

Autoplay and streaming, including the HLS/DASH segments most video sites use.

Two things worth knowing

▶️ Streaming sites already work

YouTube, Netflix, Instagram, TikTok, Twitch and other video platforms ship unblocked — you already know those cost data, and blocking them just stops them working. Want to save there too? Open one and hit Resume blocking here.
📶 On unlimited broadband?

Then you probably want ads blocked and images left alone. Open the extension and switch Block Images off — you keep the ad blocking and pages look normal again. One click, and it's where most desktop users land.

Make it yours

  1. Click the Data Saver icon in your toolbar. Can't see it? Click the puzzle-piece 🧩 and pin it.
  2. Flip any of the three switches. Changes apply immediately.
  3. Need one site to behave normally? Hit Don't block on this site at the top of the panel, or press Alt Shift D on any page. The toolbar icon shows OFF there, and nothing else changes.
  4. Want finer control? Per-site rules & history at the bottom of the panel opens the dashboard — rules for individual sites, a savings trend, and optional extras that are all switched off until you turn them on: answering cookie banners with the private option, blocking pop-ups, easing off on fast connections, and a monthly data budget.
Data Saver collects nothing. No analytics, no accounts, no server. Every blocking rule ships inside the extension and runs entirely in your browser — including the savings counter, which never leaves this device.
