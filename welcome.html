Installed & running

Data Saver is on. Pages will look different.

All three blockers start switched on, so you get the biggest possible saving straight away. That also means the web will look stripped-back until you tell it otherwise — here's what changed and how to adjust it.

What's being blocked right now

🚫 Ads & trackers Ad networks, analytics and tracking scripts. Almost nobody turns this one off.
🖼️ Images Every image, everywhere. This is the big saving — and the big visual change. Pages load as text.
🎬 Video & audio Autoplay and streaming, including the HLS/DASH segments most video sites use.
▶️ Video sites already work YouTube, Netflix, Instagram, TikTok, Twitch and other streaming platforms ship unblocked — you already know those use data, and blocking them just stops them working. Want to save data there too? Open one and hit Resume blocking here.
On unlimited broadband?

Then you probably want ads blocked and images left alone. Open the extension and switch Block Images off — you'll keep the ad blocking and the pages will look normal again. Takes one click, and it's the setting most desktop users end up on.

How to change anything

  1. Click the Data Saver icon in your toolbar. If you don't see it, click the puzzle-piece 🧩 icon and pin it.
  2. Flip any of the three switches. Changes apply straight away.
  3. Need one site to work normally? Open the extension and hit Don't block on this site — it's the first thing in the panel. Or just press Alt + Shift + D on any page. That site stays exempt, the toolbar icon shows OFF there, and your other settings are untouched.

What you're saving

The popup now keeps a running total of what's been blocked and roughly how much data that saved. It starts at zero — browse for a few minutes and check back.

Data Saver collects nothing. No analytics, no accounts, no server. Every blocking rule ships inside the extension and runs entirely in your browser — including the savings counter, which never leaves this device.
