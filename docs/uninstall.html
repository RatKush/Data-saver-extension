If one of these was the reason, there is usually a one-click fix — and if not, tell us what broke. It is the only way we find out.

A site looked broken or wouldn't work

Data Saver blocks images and video by default, which is where most of the data goes. On any site you need in full, open Data Saver and press Don't block on this site (or Alt+Shift+D). Everywhere else keeps saving.

Videos wouldn't play

YouTube, Netflix, Instagram, Twitch and other video sites are never blocked. On other sites, press Play videos on this site when the notice appears, or switch off Block Videos in the popup.

Something else

Tell us on the Chrome Web Store support page — which site, and what happened. Every report is read.
